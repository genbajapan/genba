## 1/
Champion-selling advice this year: find the internal advocate, arm them, let them build consensus. Good advice — but most of it assumes the champion's job is getting you access to more people.

In Japan, their job is getting people to agree without you in the room.

## 2/
Standard playbook: champion = door-opener. Equip them right, they get you to the economic buyer, procurement, legal.

Japan: the champion is running nemawashi on your behalf, in conversations you'll never be invited into, with arguments shaped for an internal audience — not the pitch you'd give.

## 3/
Practical shift: stop measuring champion strength by how many people they introduce you to. A strong Japan champion often introduces you to *fewer* people — because they're absorbing the consensus-building themselves instead of routing it through you.

## Last/
Ask what they need to make their internal case, not what they need from the product. Different questions. One gets you feature requests. The other gets you the real blocker.
