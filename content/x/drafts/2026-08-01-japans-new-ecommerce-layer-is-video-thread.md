## 1/
Rakuten just launched shoppable video with YouTube in Japan — the first partner for YouTube's Shopping Affiliate Program here.

If you're pitching "video commerce is Japan's chance to finally go DTC," this move is worth reading closely.

## 2/
Creators tag Rakuten Ichiba products in videos (long-form, Shorts, livestreams, posts). Viewers tap through — straight to the Rakuten product page. Rakuten's checkout, Rakuten's points, Rakuten's customer relationship.

## 3/
This isn't YouTube bypassing the marketplace. It's the marketplace absorbing video as a new discovery layer. The social-commerce argument for going direct-to-consumer just got folded back into Rakuten's ecosystem instead.

## Last/
If you sell tooling into Japanese retail/EC, the near-term buying signal is "how do we plug into Rakuten's v-commerce program" — not "how do we build our own."
