## 1/
Rakuten Travel and Jalan aren't just distribution for Japanese hotels and ryokan. They're a big part of how a first-time guest decides a property is trustworthy at all.

Same logic as the Mall Effect in retail, different vertical.

## 2/
A "help properties recover margin with direct bookings" pitch is reasonable in a lot of markets. In Japan, it risks asking a property to give up the part of the funnel that's actually earning it first-time guests.

## 3/
Better positioning: tools that work inside the OTA relationship — rate parity, review/reputation tooling on Rakuten Travel/Jalan itself — not tools that route around it.

## Last/
Reserve the direct-booking pitch for properties with existing brand recognition or a strong repeat-guest base. A first-time entrant needs the OTA's trust-transfer more than the established name does.
