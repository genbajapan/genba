## 1/
Every sales blog this year says the same thing: B2B buyers are more self-directed, in-person selling is shrinking, self-service and signal-based outreach are winning.

Apply that literally to a Japan enterprise account and watch a good deal go quiet.

## 2/
The trend is real in aggregate. But "aggregate" is doing a lot of work — it's an average across markets and deal sizes.

Japan's enterprise decision process runs on nemawashi: a champion building internal consensus for people who've never met your product. That takes relationship investment, not a lighter-touch motion.

## 3/
The failure mode isn't "using digital tactics in Japan." It's letting a global channel-mix redesign quietly become the Japan channel mix by default — under-investing in exactly the relationship work that determines whether nemawashi happens at all.

## Last/
Digital-first still earns you the first conversation faster — content, qualification, top-of-funnel. Just don't let it replace the relationship work once that conversation starts.
