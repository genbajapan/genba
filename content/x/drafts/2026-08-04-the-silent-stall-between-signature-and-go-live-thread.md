## 1/
Contract signed. HQ marks it "won." In Japan, that's not always the starting gun your onboarding clock thinks it is.

## 2/
The vendor contract itself often needs internal procurement review, IT security assessment, sometimes a separate approval from the one that got the deal signed. Legally signed ≠ cleared to actually turn the product on.

## 3/
A fast time-to-value onboarding motion starts counting from signature. If the account hasn't cleared its own internal gate yet, early nudges land on a customer who literally can't act on them — and reads as disengagement when it isn't.

## Last/
Ask about internal security/procurement timelines before signature, not after. Track the signature-to-go-live gap as its own stage, with its own expectations.
