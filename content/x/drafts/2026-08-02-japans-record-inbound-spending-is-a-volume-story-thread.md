## 1/
Japan's inbound tourism spending hit ¥9.46 trillion in 2025 — a record, up 16.4%. Per-visitor spending? Up just 0.9%.

If you're pitching yield-optimization tools into Japanese travel/hospitality, that gap matters more than the headline.

## 2/
Almost all of 2025's growth came from more visitors arriving, not each visitor spending more. That's a volume story, not a "tourists are spending more freely" story.

## 3/
At record volume with flat per-visitor spend, the acute pain for hotels and tour operators is capacity, staffing, and booking allocation — not "how do we upsell this guest."

## Last/
Yield tools aren't wrong for this market. They're just not the most urgent budget line right now. Lead with capacity, not yield.
