## 1/
Japan's cashless payment ratio just jumped from 42.8% to 58.0% in one year, per METI.

If you're about to drop that stat into a Japan market-entry deck: read the methodology footnote before you do.

## 2/
Most of that 15-point jump isn't new consumer behavior. METI rebenchmarked the National Accounts data used as the denominator in 2025 — retroactively revising housing-related figures (incl. imputed rent) upward.

That changes the ratio's base, not just its trend.

## 3/
The more honest read: real growth + a denominator change, not "Japanese consumers went cashless twice as fast as last year."

The cleaner organic signal is code/QR payments — 9.6% → 10.2% of the ratio. Smaller move, but it's actually behavior, not basis.

## Last/
Cite the number. Cite the basis with it — METI itself uses a different "international comparison indicator" for cross-country benchmarking.
