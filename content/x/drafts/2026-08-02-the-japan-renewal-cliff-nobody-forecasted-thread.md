## 1/
Your Japan pipeline can look completely healthy on a forecast call — right up until the first renewal cycle, when the account quietly churns or shrinks.

## 2/
A signed logo isn't a resolved risk. It's the post-sale version of the same "stalled but looks fine on the dashboard" problem that kills deals pre-sale — just showing up later, as churn instead of a dead deal.

## 3/
The sales cycle that won the account ran on relationship investment and internal consensus-building. If onboarding and CS revert to a generic, ticket-driven motion right after signature, that investment just stops.

## Last/
The champion who justified the purchase internally gets left to defend it alone. Their position weakens every quarter — until it's a forecast surprise.
