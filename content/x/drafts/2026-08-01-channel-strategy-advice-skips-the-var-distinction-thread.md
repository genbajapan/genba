## 1/
Most "how to enter Japan" advice treats channel partners as one decision: find a local distributor, sign them, done.

Current channel-sales thinking splits partners into 3 very different types — and Japan is where picking the wrong one costs the most.

## 2/
VARs add real services/customization, strongest for enterprise. Resellers buy and resell with minimal changes, best for geographic expansion. Referral partners (consultants, boutique advisors) have C-suite trust but don't want to run your sales cycle.

## 3/
A straight reseller is fine if your product works out of the box in Japan. It's a poor fit if you need real localization or support depth — a reseller isn't built to carry that load, and won't build it for one vendor's product.

## Last/
"Find a local partner" isn't a strategy — it's a placeholder for a decision you still have to make: distribution, services, or credibility. Match the partner type to what you actually need.
